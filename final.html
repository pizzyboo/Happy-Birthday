My dearest you,

I've been thinking about what to say in this letter for a while now. Not because I don't have enough words — it's actually the opposite. There are too many, and none of them feel big enough.

So let me start with the simplest truth I know: having you in my life is one of the best things that has ever happened to me. Not just on birthdays, not just in the big shiny moments — but in all the quiet, ordinary, everyday ones too.

You make the world a gentler, warmer, funnier, more beautiful place just by being in it. You carry kindness like other people carry keys — always with you, always ready, never making a show of it. That's rare. You are rare.

I hope this year brings you everything your heart has been softly wishing for. Moonlit moments, belly laughs, spontaneous adventures, and long peaceful evenings where everything feels exactly right. 🌸

And on the days when things feel heavy — because sometimes they will — I hope you remember that you are so deeply loved. That somewhere, someone made you a whole scrapbook just to say: you matter. You are celebrated. You are everything.

Happy Birthday. Here's to you — today, tomorrow, and every single day in between. 🎂✨🌹

you are so so so loved ♡ 🌸

With all the love in the world,

yours always 💗
